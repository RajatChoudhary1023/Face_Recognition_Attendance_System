Attendance System

How it Works?

  • Click the "Camera" Button: Initiates the attendance process.
  • Camera Activates: The system turns on the camera to scan for faces.
  • Face Detection: The system automatically detects and recognizes the face.
  • Attendance Update: Once the face is recognized above 60%, the attendance is marked as "Present."
  • Google Sheet Sync: The attendance status is updated in real-time on Google Sheets from "Absent" to "Present."
